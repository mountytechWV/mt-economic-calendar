Settings

Install as an app

Put the calendar on your home screen or taskbar so it opens in its own window like a regular app. It's the same site, so it always stays up to date.
✓ You're using the installed app.
  1. Open this page in Safari. Chrome on iPhone also works.
  2. Tap the Share button . It's at the bottom of the screen on iPhone and the top on iPad.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The icon appears on your home screen.
Always open it from that icon. Phones pause web pages in the background, so alerts only fire while the app is on screen.
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app).
  4. Tap Install. The icon appears on your home screen and in your app drawer.
Alerts only fire while the app is open on screen. Android pauses web apps in the background.
  1. Chrome: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then Install. No icon? Use the menu ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app.
  2. Edge: menu ⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app.
  3. The calendar opens in its own window. To keep it handy, right-click its taskbar icon (Windows) or Dock icon (Mac) and choose Pin / Keep in Dock.
On a computer, alerts keep working while the app is minimized or behind other windows. Turn on Desktop notifications below so they pop up over whatever you're doing.

Alerts

Alerts work while this page is open in a tab. Browsers slow down background tabs, so an alert can arrive up to a minute late if the tab has been hidden for a while. Keep the tab open (it can sit behind other windows).

Actuals & history

Actual numbers and the history charts for the major US releases come from FRED®, the St. Louis Fed's economic database. FRED usually posts a number within a few minutes of the release; for the instant print, use the Live tab.

Defaults

Everything on this panel saves automatically in this browser. Your filters save when you press Save as default at the bottom of the sidebar (or Ctrl+S).

Alerts on phones

Your alert is set, but on a phone it only fires while this app is open on screen. Phones pause web pages when you switch apps or lock the screen, so alerts can't reach you then.

  • Keep the app open around the release time, or
  • Set your alerts on a computer, where they keep working in the background.
